Pen Plotter Studio

Click the paper to place the text (capital letters, numbers and basic punctuation).
Paper and file
The canvas is your sheet of paper. Offsets say where the paper’s bottom-left corner sits on the bed. Eraser removes whole strokes. SVGs are traced as outlines (filled shapes become their outline).
Pen and printer settings
No heaters are turned on and no extrusion (E) values are written. Test the pen-down height by hand first, and keep a hand near the power switch on the first run.
